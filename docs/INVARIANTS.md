Game access policy is centralized in `canAccessGame`: lecturers always have access; students on competitive quizzes require their own completed scored attempt. Infected, Island Escape, and Memory Match are enabled. Flashcards and the three remaining future solo game types stay disabled until their individual implementation gates pass.
